# CALLSIGNAL product brief

CALLSIGNAL is a voice operations platform for teams that need to handle live support conversations with more structure than a chatbot and more control than a recording archive.

It centers the workflow around a live call session: transcript frames arrive, turns are normalized, sensitive values are redacted, intent shifts are detected, escalation rules fire, and a supervisor handoff packet is produced when the call needs another human.

The core product surfaces are the live workspace, supervisor board, call review, escalation queue, and QA center. Those views are not decorative. They are the working surfaces for the call lifecycle.
